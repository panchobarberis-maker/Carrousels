Same job. Same results. But your coworker makes $15,000 more than you.

You asked about a raise and heard "the budget is tight."
Less experienced coworkers keep getting promoted.
Nobody can explain the gap.

In Texas, paying someone less because of their race is illegal. And you don't need a smoking gun to have a case: a pattern of unequal treatment can be enough.

Signs you may have a claim:
→ You're paid less than less experienced coworkers doing the same work
→ Promotions consistently go to others
→ Raises are denied without a clear explanation

What to do now:
→ Keep your pay stubs, reviews, and any written raise or promotion requests
→ Write down dates, decisions, and who made them
→ Talk to an employment attorney before you sign anything or resign

The gap in your paycheck may be the evidence you need.
