You asked why your overtime wasn't on your paycheck.

A week later, your position was "no longer needed."

That timing is rarely a coincidence.

Employees in Texas have the right to question their pay without being punished for it. When an employer fires, demotes, or disciplines someone for raising a wage concern, that can be illegal retaliation — a claim separate from the unpaid wages themselves.

That means one situation may involve two violations, not one.

If this happened to you or someone you know:
→ Save your pay stubs and schedules
→ Write down when you raised the issue, and who you told
→ Keep any emails or texts about your hours or termination
→ Talk to an employment attorney before signing anything

Asking about your paycheck should never cost you your job.
