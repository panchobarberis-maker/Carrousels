# Skill: Lange Firm Story Carousel

Generates a 6-slide Instagram carousel (1080x1350 JPG, 4:5) telling ONE story per carousel.
Each slide = one beat of the story. Cinematic, minimal text, big impact.

## When to use

When the user asks for a "real case" or "story" carousel — one person, one situation, one outcome.

## Slide structure

- **Slide 1** — Cover: person's name + case type (e.g. "JOHN'S STORY / Disability + Retaliation")
- **Slide 2** — Chapter 1: Who they are, their job, the setup
- **Slide 3** — Chapter 2: What happened (the problem)
- **Slide 4** — Chapter 3: What they did (complaint, action)
- **Slide 5** — The Outcome: They called Evan. They won. Specific result.
- **Slide 6** — CTA: "YOUR STORY COULD END LIKE THIS." / Talk to Evan Now

## Content rules

- One story per carousel — do NOT mix multiple cases
- Each slide: 1 punchy heading (3-5 words) + 2-3 short lines of narrative
- Write like a person talking, not a lawyer writing
- Names are illustrative — not real people (John, Maria, Carlos, Sandra, etc.)
- Always Texas context — no "federal", no law names
- Outcome slide must include specific result: back pay, reinstatement, damages, etc.
- NEVER use "Free Consultation"
- CTA: "Talk to Evan Now" / "Contact Us for a Consultation"
- Logo on every slide: THE LANGE FIRM

## Brand colors

- Navy: #1B2B4A (overlays and CTA background)
- Bronze: #9B6820 (THE LANGE in logo)
- Gold: #C9A84C (FIRM, arrows, accents, deco lines)
- Gold light: #E2C97E (large stat numbers, outcome text)
- White: #F0F4FF

## Story ideas (illustrative, not real cases)

- John: Disability accommodation denied → fired after complaining → won disability + retaliation case
- Maria: Pregnant → hours cut → forced out → won pregnancy discrimination case
- Carlos: Tips stolen by manager for years → won wage theft case
- Sandra: Passed over for promotion twice due to race → won discrimination case
- Ana: Sexually harassed → HR ignored her → constructive dismissal → won hostile workplace case
