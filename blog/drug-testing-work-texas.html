Drug Testing at Work in Texas: Your Rights Before and After a Test

Short answer

Yes, Texas private employers can generally drug test job applicants and employees, and they can fire employees who test positive for illegal drugs. Under the ADA, a test for illegal drugs isn't a medical exam, and current illegal drug use isn't protected. But there are limits: employers generally can't demand a list of all your lawful prescription medications, people in or who have completed recovery programs who no longer use drugs illegally are protected from discrimination, and testing can't be applied selectively based on race, sex or another protected trait. For unemployment, the Texas Workforce Commission requires an employer to prove a positive test with a written policy, your consent, chain of custody and lab confirmation.

Pre-employment screens, random tests in safety-sensitive jobs, post-accident tests: drug testing is common across Houston's energy, construction, transportation and healthcare industries. Most testing is legal. What matters is how it's done and what happens next.

👉 Employers can test. They can't use a test as a cover for discrimination or a fishing trip into your medical history.

Workers in DOT-regulated jobs, like commercial truck drivers, have federal testing requirements on top of company policy.

2Prescriptions, Disability and Recovery

  • Prescription medication: using a controlled substance as prescribed by a doctor isn't illegal drug use. If a test flags your medication, you can explain it, often to a medical review officer.
  • Medication questions: broad questions about all the prescription drugs you take are generally disability-related inquiries the ADA restricts.
  • Recovery: people who no longer use drugs illegally and are in or have completed a rehabilitation program are protected from discrimination based on past addiction. Testing to confirm you've stopped is allowed.
  • Timing matters: entering rehab right after a positive test generally doesn't protect you from discipline for that test.

3Marijuana, CBD and Hemp Products

Marijuana remains illegal under Texas law except for limited low-THC medical use under the Texas Compassionate Use Program, and employers can generally still enforce drug-free workplace policies. Some CBD and hemp products can contain THC and cause a positive test, so check labels carefully if your job tests.

4Failed Test and Unemployment

A positive test can be "misconduct" that disqualifies you from unemployment, but the Texas Workforce Commission requires the employer to prove it. TWC guidance says the employer generally needs:

  1. A written drug policy you knew about
  2. Your consent to the test
  3. A documented chain of custody for the sample
  4. Lab confirmation of the result
  5. A result above the stated cutoff

TWC also says employers that fire based only on one initial screen will almost always lose if the employee denies drug use. See collecting unemployment if you were fired and termination for cause.

5When Drug Testing Is Illegal

  • Only employees of one race, sex, age or national origin are singled out for "random" tests
  • A test is ordered right after you complained about harassment, wages or safety
  • You were fired over a prescribed medication without a chance to explain
  • Your employer shared your results with coworkers

?Frequently Asked Questions

Can my employer drug test me in Texas?

Generally yes. Texas private employers can drug test applicants and employees, including random testing under a written policy, as long as testing is not applied in a discriminatory or retaliatory way.

Can I be fired for a positive drug test in Texas?

Usually yes, for illegal drug use. Current illegal drug use is not protected by the ADA. Lawful use of a prescribed medication is treated differently, and you should explain it to the employer or medical review officer.

Can I get unemployment after failing a drug test in Texas?

Possibly. The Texas Workforce Commission requires the employer to prove a written policy, your consent, chain of custody, lab confirmation and a result above the cutoff. Employers relying only on an initial screen often lose.

Are people in drug recovery protected at work?

Yes. People who no longer use drugs illegally and are in or have completed a rehabilitation program are protected from discrimination based on past addiction, though employers may test to confirm they are not using.

Can CBD cause a positive drug test?

It can. Some CBD and hemp products contain THC, which may cause a positive result, and employers can generally enforce drug-free policies.
