How We Help Houston Employees

Every employment case is different. Learn more about the situations we handle most often:

  • Wrongful Termination – Fired for reporting misconduct, taking protected leave, or because of discrimination.
  • Workplace Harassment – Harassment based on race, sex, age, disability, religion, or another protected characteristic.
  • Workplace Discrimination – Unequal treatment in hiring, pay, promotion, or termination.
  • EEOC Claims – Filing a charge with the EEOC or Texas Workforce Commission before the deadline.
