Fired for Calling in Sick in Texas? When It's Legal and When It Isn't

Short answer

Sometimes. Texas is an at-will state, so an employer can generally discipline or fire you for missing work with an ordinary illness like a cold or the flu, especially under a neutral attendance policy. But firing you for calling in sick is illegal if the absence was protected: FMLA leave for a serious health condition (at employers with 50+ employees, if you're eligible), a disability under the ADA, a pregnancy-related condition, a work injury covered by workers' comp, or if the "sick day" was used as an excuse to retaliate or discriminate.

You wake up sick, follow your employer's call-in rules, and stay home. A few days later, you're fired. It's one of the most common situations Houston employees bring to an employment lawyer. Whether it's legal comes down to why you were sick, how big your employer is, and what really drove the decision.

1When Firing You for Calling in Sick Is Usually Legal

  • Short, ordinary illnesses like a cold, stomach bug or the flu, when no protected leave applies
  • Breaking a neutral call-in or attendance policy that applies to everyone, such as not calling in before your shift

3What Counts as a "Serious Health Condition"

Under the FMLA, a serious health condition generally involves an overnight hospital stay, or continuing treatment by a health care provider, such as an incapacity of more than three days with treatment, a chronic condition like asthma or diabetes, or pregnancy. A routine cold usually doesn't qualify; a flu with complications, surgery or a chronic flare-up might.

You don't need to say "FMLA"

You only have to give your employer enough information to suggest the absence may be FMLA-qualifying. It's then up to the employer to ask for more details or medical certification, which you generally have at least 15 calendar days to provide.

4Don't Give Them an Easy Reason

Even when an absence is protected, employers often point to a procedural slip. Following the call-in rules matters: under FMLA regulations, you generally must follow your employer's usual call-in procedures unless unusual circumstances prevent it. If a doctor's note was requested, turn it in on time. See whether a doctor's note is enough.

5Signs the Firing Was Really About Something Else

  • You were fired after one sick day, while others with more absences weren't
  • The firing came soon after you disclosed a diagnosis, pregnancy or disability
  • You had recently complained about harassment, discrimination, unpaid overtime or safety
  • The reason changed from "attendance" to "performance" after the fact

Those can point to retaliation or disability discrimination.

✅What to Do If You Were Fired for Calling in Sick

  1. Save your call-in texts or emails and any doctor's notes.
  2. Get the attendance policy and your termination notice.
  3. Write down the timeline: diagnosis, leave requests, complaints, how others were treated.
  4. Apply for unemployment: absences for illness are often not misconduct. See unemployment if you were fired.
  5. Talk to an employment lawyer and watch deadlines: generally 180 days with the TWC or 300 with the EEOC for discrimination claims, and two years for most FMLA claims.

?Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get fired for calling in sick in Texas?

Sometimes. Texas is an at-will state with no private paid sick leave law, so employers can generally fire employees for ordinary illness absences. It is illegal if the absence was protected by the FMLA, the ADA or pregnancy laws, involved a workers' comp claim, or was used as an excuse for retaliation or discrimination.

Can I be fired for calling in sick one time?

Possibly, if no protected leave applies and the employer follows a neutral policy. But firing you for a single absence when others with more absences were kept, or right after a complaint or medical disclosure, can be evidence of discrimination or retaliation.

Does FMLA protect me if I'm sick?

If you are eligible and have a serious health condition, yes. Eligible employees at employers with 50 or more employees can take up to 12 weeks of job-protected leave, and firing them for using it is illegal.

Is it illegal to fire someone for having a disability that causes absences?

It can be. Under the ADA, an employer with 15 or more employees may have to provide leave or a schedule change as a reasonable accommodation, and cannot fire someone because of a disability.

How long do I have to take action after being fired for calling in sick?

Discrimination charges generally must be filed within 180 days with the Texas Workforce Commission or 300 days with the EEOC. Most FMLA lawsuits must be filed within two years, or three years for willful violations.
